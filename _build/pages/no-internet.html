Runs with no internet

Camera AI that runs with no internet

Detection, counting and review run on a computer on your site. Nothing on site has to reach the internet. Retraining happens in our cloud, or in yours, and comes back as a signed update that a person approves.

What runs on your site

  • Detection and counting on your cameras' live or recorded video.
  • Tracking, so each item is counted once as it moves.
  • The review queue. Mismatches go to a person with the clip.
  • Writing results into the systems you already run.
  • Nightly reports.

At Healthfields, five services and four nightly reports run on one computer on site, and keep running with no internet.

How it keeps improving

  • It spots its own misses. Counts are compared with your records and with a person's sample.
  • Retraining happens off site, in our cloud by agreement, or in your own cloud account if your images cannot leave it.
  • The update arrives as a signed file. Your computer checks the signature before it installs anything.
  • A person approves it. Nothing deploys itself.

Offline is not the same as running offline

Many vision tools can run a model without a connection once its weights are stored. With most of them, the rest of the job still happens in the cloud: reviewing results, checking them against your records, and fixing the model when the site changes. VisorVolt runs that whole job on site. Only the retraining leaves it.
