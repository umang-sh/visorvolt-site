Vision AI that runs with no internet

Detection, counting and review run on a computer on your site. Nothing on site has to reach the internet. Retraining happens in our cloud, or in yours, and comes back as a signed update that a person approves.

What runs on your site

  • Detection and counting on your cameras’ live or recorded video.
  • Tracking, so each item is counted once as it moves.
  • The review queue. Mismatches go to a person with the video clip.
  • Writing results into the systems you already run, such as a warehouse or maintenance system.
  • Nightly reports.

At Healthfields, five services and four nightly reports run on one computer on site, and keep running with no internet.

How it keeps improving

  1. It spots its own misses. The improvement agent compares counts with your records and flags where the model is unsure.
  2. Retraining happens off site. In our cloud by agreement, or in your own cloud account if your images cannot leave it.
  3. The update arrives as a signed file. Your computer checks the signature before it installs anything.
  4. A person approves it. Nothing deploys itself: a human approves every promotion.

Offline inference is not the same as running offline

Many vision tools can run a model without a connection once its weights are cached. With most of them, the rest of the job happens in the cloud: reviewing results, checking them against your records, and fixing the model when the site changes. VisorVolt runs that whole job on site. Only the retraining leaves it.
