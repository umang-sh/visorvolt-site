What it means for you

  • One project, one team. Prolifics’ delivery teams connect VisorVolt to the systems you already run, such as your warehouse, maintenance or ERP system. We supply the vision platform and the improvement agent that keeps each model current.
  • Any industry. The same engine learns pallets, cartons, antennas or defects. There is no separate product per vertical.
  • No labelling project on your side. 40 images per site, and we pick the 40 from footage you already record, then label them ourselves.
  • Runs on your hardware. Counting and review run on site with no internet. Retraining happens in our cloud and arrives as a signed update. How that works.

How an engagement runs

  1. Scope. Prolifics and VisorVolt agree with you what to detect or count, on which cameras, and where the results go.
  2. 40 images per site. We pick them from your recordings and label them.
  3. Proof of Value. A short trial on your own site, with success criteria agreed up front.
  4. Live, and improving. Results flow into your systems. The improvement agent finds misses and proposes fixes, and a person approves every change.

Already running in production

At Healthfields, VisorVolt counts cartons at dispatch and on the packing line with the existing cameras and writes the counts into the warehouse system. Cartons came within 1% of a person’s per-carton count on a day the model never saw.
