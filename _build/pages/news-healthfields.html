Healthfields counts cartons with VisorVolt on the cameras it already has

Healthfields counts cartons at dispatch and on its packing line with VisorVolt, on its existing cameras and with no internet. Dated results v a person.

Healthfields, a maker of organic staples with more than 600 products, uses VisorVolt at its site in Greater Noida, India. VisorVolt counts what leaves on the site's existing CCTV cameras and writes the counts into the warehouse system. On a dispatch day the system had never seen, the camera counted 102 cartons and a person counted 103. On a packing line over two unseen days, the camera counted 141 and a person counted 141 over 38 sampled windows. Counting and review run on one computer on site, with no internet. The deployment is ongoing and the case study is kept up to date.
