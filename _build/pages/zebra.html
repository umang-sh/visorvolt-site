What VisorVolt adds to a warehouse or plant

  • Counts from the cameras you have. Cartons, sacks, pallets and packed goods counted at the dock, the packing line and dispatch.
  • Into the system that acts on it. Counts are written into your warehouse system, and mismatches go to review with the video clip.
  • Camera counts and barcode scans side by side. At Healthfields, camera counts and scans are reconciled every day into one stock position.
  • No internet needed on site. Counting and review run on a computer on your site. How updates arrive.
