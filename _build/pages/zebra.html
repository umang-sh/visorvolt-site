VisorVolt is a Zebra Technologies Registered ISV

VisorVolt is a Registered ISV in Zebra Technologies' PartnerConnect program, in North America and Asia-Pacific. For sites that already run on Zebra, it is a route to camera checkpoints through the partners they already buy from.

What VisorVolt adds to a site that runs on Zebra

  • Counts from the cameras you have. Cartons and pallets counted at the dock, the packing line and dispatch.
  • Into the system that acts on it. Counts are written into your warehouse system, and mismatches go to review with the clip.
  • Camera counts and barcode scans side by side. At Healthfields, camera counts and scans are compared every day.
  • No internet needed on site. Counting and review run on a computer on your site. How updates arrive
  • Audited. A person counts a random sample and compares it with the camera.
