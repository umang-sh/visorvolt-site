VisorVolt becomes a Zebra Technologies Registered ISV

VisorVolt joined Zebra Technologies’ PartnerConnect program as a Registered ISV, in North America and Asia-Pacific.

Redwood City, CA, {{date:zebra}}. VisorVolt is now a Registered ISV in Zebra Technologies’ PartnerConnect program, under agreements covering North America and Asia-Pacific.

VisorVolt counts cartons, sacks, pallets and packed goods on the cameras a site already has, and writes the counts into its warehouse system. It learns a new site from 40 images, which VisorVolt picks and labels itself, and runs on the site’s own hardware with no internet.

“Warehouses and plants already run on Zebra. As a Registered ISV, VisorVolt is easier to find through the partners they already work with,” said Umang Sharma, founder and CEO of VisorVolt.

About VisorVolt

VisorVolt builds vision AI that learns a new site from 40 images and keeps improving after it ships. Counting and inspection run on the customer’s own hardware with no internet, an improvement agent keeps every model current, and a person approves every change. Across 39 public datasets, the method reaches a median 89% of the accuracy of a fully hand-labeled model. VisorVolt is based in Redwood City, California. visorvolt.ai
