Healthfields counts cartons on the cameras it already had

● Running since October 2026 · Last updated 20 November 2026

Healthfields makes and sells organic staples, with more than 600 products. At its site in Greater Noida, VisorVolt counts what leaves on the existing CCTV cameras, and writes the counts into the warehouse system. It runs on one computer on site, with no internet needed.

This is an ongoing deployment. Every number below is development-stage evidence, not a final figure for any use case.

The camera counted 141 cartons and a person counted 141, over 38 sampled windows on two days the system had never seen. 23 windows matched exactly; the range is about 7% either way.

On a day the model had never seen, the camera counted 102 cartons loaded at the dock and a person counting each carton counted 103.

The challenge

Stock moves through many doors. Goods arrive by truck, finished goods are packed into cartons, and dispatch loads them onto trucks. Counting by hand is slow, and every count has to agree with barcode scans and registers. Healthfields wanted counts it could trust, from the cameras it already owned, without sending its footage off site.

What we built

  • Vehicle gate and truck triggers. A camera spots a truck at the bay and starts the count. Trained from about 40 frames.
  • Dispatch counter. A detector built from 40 labelled images, with a tracker, counts each carton loaded at dispatch.
  • Packing line counter. A running count of cartons at the end of the line.
  • Into the warehouse system. Counts are written into the warehouse system. Mismatches go to review with the clip. Camera counts and barcode scans are compared every day.

One computer on site runs five services and four nightly reports, and keeps running with no internet.

How we measured

A person counts, without relying on the camera's answer, and we compare. For dispatch, the person counted each carton loaded on one truck. For the packing line, random windows were drawn from two days (18 and 19 November 2026) and counted by eye. The version under test was fixed before those days.

Earlier versions over-counted on random windows. Those results are why the version under test was changed before these days.
