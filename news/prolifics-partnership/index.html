VisorVolt and Prolifics sign a systems integrator partnership

Prolifics, a global systems integrator with 2,000+ experts, will deliver VisorVolt vision AI to its clients, across every industry.

Redwood City, CA, 26 October 2026. VisorVolt today announced a partnership agreement with Prolifics, a global provider of AI services and data and digital engineering with 2,000+ experts in 10 locations. Under the agreement, Prolifics delivers VisorVolt to its clients and connects it to the systems they already run.

The partnership is not tied to one industry. VisorVolt learns a new site from 40 images, which VisorVolt picks and labels itself, so the same engine can count goods in a warehouse, find assets on a grid, or inspect equipment on a tower. Counting and review run on the client’s own hardware with no internet, and an improvement agent keeps each model current after it ships.

“Prolifics brings delivery teams and long client relationships. We bring a platform that learns a new site from 40 images and keeps getting better once it is live,” said Umang Sharma, founder and CEO of VisorVolt. “Together we can put vision AI into production in any industry, without a labelling project on the client’s side.”

VisorVolt is already live in production at Healthfields, where it counts cartons on the existing cameras.
