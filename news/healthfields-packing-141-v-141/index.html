Healthfields packing line: 141 cartons counted by camera, 141 by a person

Over 38 sampled windows on two days the system had never seen, the camera and a person agreed on the total.

Redwood City, CA, 25 November 2026. VisorVolt counted 141 cartons by camera on a Healthfields packing line, and a person counted 141, over 38 sampled windows on 18 and 19 November 2026. Both days were new to the system, and the version under test was fixed before them. 23 of the 38 windows matched exactly, and the range the true total could fall in is about 7% either way.

Healthfields is an ongoing deployment, so this is development-stage evidence and not a final figure. On a dispatch day the system had never seen, the camera counted 102 cartons loaded at the dock and a person counted 103.
