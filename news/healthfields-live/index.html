Healthfields counts cartons with VisorVolt, live in production

Healthfields counts cartons at dispatch and on the packing line with its existing cameras, on site with no internet. The case study is kept up to date.

Redwood City, CA, 26 October 2026. Healthfields, a maker of organic staples with more than 600 products, is live in production with VisorVolt at its site in Greater Noida, India. VisorVolt counts what arrives and what leaves on the site’s existing CCTV cameras and writes the counts into the warehouse system.

Measured on a day the model never saw, the carton counter came within 1% of a person’s per-carton count (102 against 103), and the packing counter was exact on all 7 hand-counted checks. Counting and review run on one computer on site, with no internet needed.

“Healthfields is the proof we wanted: counting on cameras they already had, with no internet, measured on days the models never saw,” said Umang Sharma, founder and CEO of VisorVolt.

The deployment is still changing every week. Follow it in the live case study.
