Prolifics, a global systems integrator with 2,000+ experts in 10 locations, delivers VisorVolt to its clients. The partnership covers every industry, from factories and warehouses to ports, construction, telecom and energy.

What it means for you

  • One project, one team. Prolifics' delivery teams connect VisorVolt to the systems you already run, such as a warehouse, maintenance or ERP system. We supply the checkpoint platform and the improvement agent that keeps each model current.
  • Any industry. The same method builds a gate, a dock, a line or a door. There is no separate product per vertical.
  • No labelling project on your side. We pick about 40 images from footage you already record and label them ourselves.
  • Runs on your site. Counting and review run on a computer on your site with no internet. How that works

How an engagement runs

  1. Scope. Prolifics and VisorVolt agree with you what to count or check, on which cameras, and where results go.
  2. Build. We pick about 40 images from your recordings and label them.
  3. Proof of value. A short trial on your own site, with success criteria agreed up front.
  4. Live and audited. Results flow into your systems, a person audits a sample, and a person approves every change.

Already running

At Healthfields, VisorVolt counts cartons at dispatch and on the packing line, using the cameras already there. On a day it had never seen, the camera counted 102 cartons at dispatch and a person counted 103. On a packing line over two unseen days, 141 by camera and 141 by a person over 38 sampled windows. Healthfields is ongoing; these are development-stage results.
