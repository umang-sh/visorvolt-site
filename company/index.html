About VisorVolt

VisorVolt turns any camera checkpoint, in any industry, into a count or a check that a person audits. It is built for each site, runs on the site with no internet, and improves every month.

Every operation has checkpoints. Nobody independent checks them.

At a gate, a dock, the end of a line, someone writes down a number or ticks a box, and money or a decision depends on it. VisorVolt puts a camera on the checkpoint, builds the count or check for that site, and has a person audit a random sample. Each row keeps its clip, so anyone can see what the count was based on.

For investors

The market, in plain words

Every industry has places where something is counted, logged or checked by hand, and where a payment, a claim or a delivery depends on the answer. Cameras are already there. What is missing is a count that someone independent has audited and that comes with its evidence.

The model, in plain words

We build each checkpoint for the customer's site, charge per site and per checkpoint, and the customer buys the computer it runs on. Systems integrators and technology partners carry us into their clients.

Why it can be one company across industries

Our research found the site matters far more than the industry. Forty images from a site's own footage beat thousands from elsewhere. The same method builds a gate, a line or a dock, so a checkpoint built in one industry can be built in the next.

What is true today

One customer running it and measured against a person, one integrator, one registered ISV, published research, one founder.

What we have not shown yet

The same checkpoint holding its accuracy in a second industry, and accuracy improving month after month. We say so because both are what we are working on next.
